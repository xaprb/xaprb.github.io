---
title: Birding at Boxerwood
---
Boxerwood bird walks take place at 8:00 AM on the second Saturday morning of every month.

These member-led walks are a great introduction for
beginning birders, and an opportunity for already avid
birders to track the local phenology--those seasonal
changes in nature relating to weather and climate,
including bird migration.

As we stroll Boxerwood’s fields and woodlands this
Fall, we’ll be looking and listening for species migrating
south and those returning early to winter in Rockbridge.
Walks begin and end in the nature center parking lot at
8am and run approximately two hours. Bring binoculars
and wear sturdy shoes. We usually have binoculars available for those who don’t have
their own. For more information, including rain cancellations, visit Rockbridge Bird Club on
Facebook.
