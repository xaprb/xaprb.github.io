---
title: Around Town Bird Walk
---
Have you ever wondered how many bird
species inhabit the developed town of
Lexington compared to surrounding "wild"
areas? Or maybe you’d like to do something
relaxing after the Thanksgiving holiday – or
just want to burn off some of the pumpkin pie?

For whatever reason, join RBC members at
8:30 a.m. the Saturday after Thanksgiving at
Richardson Park (300 Diamond Street) to walk
around town spotting some of Lexington’s
resident birds. We’ll begin birdwatching in the
park, then stroll down a few city streets to see
what other feathered friends might be wintering in our “urban” habitats. The walk will go no
more than two hours, and participants can drop out at any time.

Wear comfortable shoes, bring binoculars, and dress for the weather. For more information
or a possible rain or snow cancellation, check Rockbridge Bird Club on Facebook.

John Maluski, organizer.
