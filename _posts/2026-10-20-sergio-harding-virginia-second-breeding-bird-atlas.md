Takeaways from the 2nd Breeding Bird Atlas
Presentation by DWR Conservation Biologist
Sergio Harding on Oct 20

Rockbridge Bird Club will welcome Sergio Harding, Nongame
Bird Conservation Biologist with the Virginia Department of
Wildlife Resources (DWR), to speak at our first program for
the 2026-27 calendar year. Sergio will give a presentation on
Virginia’s Second Breeding Bird Atlas in the Maury River
Middle School Library at 7pm on October 20.

The Atlas is among the largest avian survey projects to take
place in the Commonwealth, with close to 1,500 volunteers
having contributed data between 2016 and 2020 – including
several RBC members. An Atlas website summarizing these
data was published in October 2025, with additional content
published in August 2026. Sergio’s presentation will highlight
the Atlas data products and website, sharing key takeaways
of what we have learned and how this information will
contribute to bird conservation in Virginia.

Harding has worked as a nongame bird conservation biologist with DWR since 2005. He
oversees various avian projects, including the Atlas, and coordinates with other agencies
and organizations on bird conservation issues. He is also the Virginia coordinator of the
North American Breeding Bird Survey (BBS). His field work has included breeding land bird
surveys of wildlife management areas and surveys of bald eagles, peregrine falcons,
golden-winged warblers, loggerhead shrikes and breeding marsh birds.
