---
title: Harvey's Knob Hawk Watch
---

Join the Hawk Watch at Harvey’s Knob on Sept 26. 
Raptors migrate in the fall along the
Blue Ridge flight corridor, and we’re
fortunate to have two Hawk Watches
within reach: Rockfish Gap on Afton
Mountain and Harvey’s Knob, south
of Buchanan on the Parkway.

Expert educators and committed
Hawk Watch counters are at the
sites daily from mid-August through
early November. Peak numbers
occur in mid- to late September, due
to the very large numbers of Broad-winged Hawks. Osprey and Bald
Eagle are also in flight over the ridges in mid-September.

Join fellow Club members as we participate in the count at Harvey’s Knob on September
26, starting at 10am. Take Route 43 east from Buchanan for 4 miles and hop onto the
Parkway heading south. The overlook is located at milepost 95.3, offering spectacular
views as raptors kettle over the Blue Ridge.

Bring binoculars or a spotting scope, a chair, sunscreen, drinks and snacks, and a hat for
hawk-watching, and stay as long as you like. You can also keep up with daily raptor counts
for both the Harvey’s Knob and Rockfish Gap sites on [HawkCount](https://www.hawkcount.org/), a raptor migration
database.
