---
title: Birding at Boxerwood
---
Boxerwood bird walks take place on the second Saturday morning. 
In November and December we will start at 8:30 AM, giving birds
and birders an extra half hour to warm up.

These member-led walks are a great introduction for
beginning birders, and an opportunity for already avid
birders to track the local phenology--those seasonal
changes in nature relating to weather and climate,
including bird migration.

Target species for early winter include Hermit Thrush, White-
throated Sparrow, Yellow-bellied Sapsucker, and Winter
Wren. Boxerwood birders spotted their FOS (first of the
season) White-throat along with the usual suspects on our
October walk.

Birders meet in the nature center parking lot for a two-hour
stroll through the field and woodlands. Bring binoculars,
wear sturdy shoes, and dress for weather. Walks are
canceled for heavy rain or snow. For more information or
possible weather cancellation, check Rockbridge Bird Club
on Facebook.
